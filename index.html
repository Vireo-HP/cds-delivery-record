Chain of custody: This record must be retained at the pharmacy for a minimum of 2 years. Any loss or theft of a CDS in transit must be reported to the College of Pharmacists of BC within 24 hours and to Health Canada within 10 business days (CDSA s.56; RS26-010 s.14).
Delivery staff: Must hold a valid criminal record check, B.C. driving abstract (if applicable), and current OAT awareness training on file at the pharmacy (HPOA Sch. E.14 s.12; RS26-010 s.10–11).
Undelivered doses: Record reason, return CDS to pharmacy, and notify the pharmacist on duty immediately.
